About Us

Helping People With Hope And Dignity

CCF is committed to helping people and supporting communities through practical assistance, compassion and collective action.

CCF works to support people who need help and to encourage stronger communities through compassion, respect and action.

Our mission is to provide meaningful help and support to people in need while promoting dignity, hope and opportunity.

Our vision is a stronger community where people can live with hope, dignity and a better opportunity for the future.

We believe in respect, integrity, teamwork, compassion and creating meaningful positive impact.
